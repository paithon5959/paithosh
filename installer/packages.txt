# Luu y ve KDE Connect (bat buoc, chi su dung CLI `kdeconnect-cli`, khong can mo app GUI):

#   - Tren Arch / Debian / Ubuntu: goi duy nhat la `kdeconnect` (da bao gom `kdeconnect-cli`).
